01 · About

I'm a robotics engineer building the perception and decision-making that let a vehicle drive itself — from a 1:10-scale race car to a full-size autonomous EV on campus roads.

I'm a B.Tech. student in Civil Engineering at IIT Bombay (minoring in AI & Data Science), but most of my time goes into Team SeDriCa, IIT Bombay's self-driving car initiative, where I lead a 40-person team building India's first Level 4 autonomous vehicle for Indian roads — currently running at Level 3 autonomy at 30 km/h in auto-drive mode. My own work sits mostly in perception, sensor fusion, and reinforcement learning: fusing LiDAR, depth cameras, IMU and GNSS through an EKF, training RL policies for obstacle avoidance and scenario generation, and shipping the ROS2 stacks that turn that into a vehicle that actually moves. I've carried that into a research internship generating safety-critical driving scenarios with Mercedes-Benz Research, a fleet-management internship at Armstrong Dematic, and an international outing at the Roboracer autonomous-racing competition in Busan, South Korea.

02 · Experience

Where I've built this

  1. Sep 2024 — Present

    Team Lead & Perception Subsystem Lead · Self Driving Car (SeDriCa), IIT Bombay

    Leading a 40+ member, cross-functional team (₹4M+ budget) building India's first Level 4 self-driving car for Indian roads — achieved Level 3 autonomy at 30 km/h in auto-drive mode. Own the perception subsystem: multi-LiDAR + depth-camera environmental mapping, RGB-D scene understanding with U-Net and RTAB-Map, YOLOv8 road segmentation and dynamic-obstacle detection, and EKF sensor fusion across IMU, GNSS, odometry and point clouds.

  2. Aug 2025 — Apr 2026

    Research Intern · Mercedes-Benz Research

    Built an RL-driven tool to generate, rank, and export safety-critical AD/ADAS scenarios in OpenSCENARIO/CARLA. Architected a 5-layer ODD scenario generator with semantic feasibility constraints, and trained a PPO policy (Stable-Baselines3) on 10K+ preview and ~300 closed-loop CARLA episodes — beating random search by 25%+ on a 7-metric Composite Criticality Score. Integrated schema-constrained LLM parsing for OpenSCENARIO export with a CMA-ES fallback, plus real-world crash grounding via contrastive embeddings and KNN retrieval.

  3. Mar 2026 — May 2026

    Robotics Software Development Intern · Armstrong Dematic

    Built a ROS2 fleet-management system for multi-robot warehouse navigation, integrating Nav2 and Gazebo. Redesigned zone-tracking to check only what changed per update, cutting computation by 86% to O(1) map-wide complexity. Wired cross-track-error deviation detection into Behavior Trees for automated safety control.

  4. Mar 2025 — Apr 2026

    Perception Subsystem Lead · Team SeDriCa

    Explored ResNet and vision-language models for perception robustness under motion blur, low-texture scenes, and dynamic obstacles — hardening the same RGB-D pipeline that drives the full-scale vehicle's road understanding.

03 · Projects

What I've built

A vehicle that drives itself doesn't happen at one scale. These span a 1:10 race car, a full-size EV, a warehouse fleet, a robotic arm, and the RL/planning work underneath all of them.

Team SeDriCa

Full-Scale Autonomous Vehicle

A Level 3 autonomous electric vehicle running at 30 km/h in auto-drive mode on campus roads — the flagship of Team SeDriCa. Multi-LiDAR and depth-camera perception, YOLOv8 road segmentation and dynamic-obstacle detection, and IMU/GNSS/odometry/LiDAR fusion through an EKF for real-time state estimation.

Team SeDriCa

F1TENTH — Perception & Motion Planning

The 1:10-scale testbed where SeDriCa's autonomy stack gets proven before it reaches the full-size car: the perception pipeline and motion-planning algorithms that decide where the vehicle can safely go, at a scale where mistakes are cheap to iterate on.

Bosch Competition

Urban Scenario Navigation

An urban-driving demonstration built for a Bosch-hosted competition — intersections and dynamic obstacles handled by the same perception-to-control pipeline developed for SeDriCa's urban autonomy stack.

International

Roboracer — Busan, South Korea

Represented IIT Bombay at the International Roboracer Autonomous Racing Competition (IFAC) as part of a 5-member contingent, leading development of a 1:10-scale autonomous race car — driving system design and technical decision-making. Secured 3rd place internationally.

Armstrong Dematic

Multi-Robot Fleet Management

ROS2 fleet-management system for multi-robot warehouse navigation (Nav2 + Gazebo), with real-time zone-occupancy tracking optimized to O(1) per update — an 86% cut in computation — and cross-track-error deviation detection wired into Behavior Trees for automated safety stops.

BTech Project

Multi-Agent RL for Robotic Patrolling

Graph-based patrol coverage with PPO multi-agent RL — GAT encoding over a NetworkX/GraphML map with MAT autoregressive decoding for synchronous and asynchronous agent decisions. 65% lower idleness and 32% higher entropy than baseline patrol strategies, with OR-Tools TSP/mTSP planners for workload-balanced coverage. Guide: Prof. Leena Vachhani, Centre for Systems and Control.

Tinkerers' Laboratory

6-DOF Robotic Arm

Spearheaded a team of 5 to build a robotic arm with a modifiable end effector on ROS2, Gazebo and RViz. Modelled the dynamics in MATLAB Robotics Toolbox, designed the structure in SolidWorks, and added YOLO + OpenCV object detection for real-time visual recognition and automated pick tasks.

Seasons of Code

Deep RL for Real-Time Obstacle Avoidance

A TD3 (Twin-Delayed DDPG) policy trained for autonomous mobile-robot collision avoidance — a 47% improvement in cumulative reward, validated by tracking Q-value trends for stable learning. Adapted into a ROS pipeline with SLAM Toolbox for real-time mapping.

Inter-IIT Tech Meet 14.0

Built for Eternal's autonomous rover challenge at Inter-IIT Tech Meet 14.0, competing against every other IIT. Won the silver medal.

InnovationFair 2026

HumanityOS — Tele-operated Assistive Arm

A tele-operated, semi-autonomous assistive robotic arm combining overhead RGB-D sensing, edge-AI scene understanding, and gesture-driven control over ROS2 + MoveIt2 — deployed on an STM32MP257F heterogeneous core. Selected as a National Finalist, securing an STM32 hardware grant.
